How it works

  1. Choose your package and send us your requirements (valid ID, postal address, email address — plus section and allotment for companies).
  2. Pay in full to start. We lodge your IPA and IRC applications and follow them up for you.
  3. Receive your documents — certificate, extract, TIN, profile, logo, letterhead and bank letter — usually within 2–3 weeks.

Clients anywhere in Papua New Guinea can use our services — most work is done remotely or via courier.
