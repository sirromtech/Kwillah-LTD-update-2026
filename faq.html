What services does Kwilah Ltd offer?

We offer business registration (IPA & IRC), branding (logos, business profiles, letterheads), bank setup assistance, tax compliance support and business advisory services. We also provide building and construction, fisheries and agribusiness services.

How long does it take to register a business?

Registration typically takes 2–3 weeks depending on IPA and IRC processing times. We handle all submissions and follow-ups to ensure timely delivery.

What’s included in the Company Combo Package?

The K850 package includes an IPA Certificate & Extract, IRC TIN, Company Profile, Custom Logo, Letterhead Design and a Bank Letter. The K550 Business Name Combo Package includes the same items with a Business Profile.

What do I need to register?

For a business name you need a valid ID, a postal address and an email address. For a company you also need the section and allotment of the business location.

Can I get help with tax returns or compliance?

Yes. We assist with tax registration, compliance and advisory to ensure your business meets IRC requirements.

Do you offer services outside of Port Moresby?

Absolutely. We serve clients across Papua New Guinea, including remote areas. Most services are delivered digitally or via courier.
